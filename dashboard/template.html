Turn segments into experiments

Active frequent & active repeat

Prioritise reliable service and relevant replenishment reminders. Monetary value is gross spend before credits.

Lapsed repeat

Previously ordered at least twice, but no purchase in over 90 days. Test reactivation against a holdout group.

Recent one-time

One observed order within 30 days. Test a second-purchase message. These are not confirmed new customers.

Monthly cohort retention

Share purchasing in each month after first observed purchase. M0 is the cohort month. “—” means unavailable, not zero. Partial December 2011 is excluded.

Segment rules and customer limitations

Applied in order: Active frequent ≤30 days and ≥5 orders; Active repeat ≤90 days and ≥2 orders; Recent one-time ≤30 days and 1 order; Lapsed repeat >90 days and ≥2 orders; all others Other customers. Recency uses calendar days from 10 Dec 2011. Anonymous customers are excluded. First observed purchase may not be acquisition. Repeat share is measured over the available window, not lifetime.

Rows from source to analysis

Exact duplicates are assumed to be duplicate ingestion. Without a line ID, that assumption cannot be proven. No outliers are trimmed.

Monetary reconciliation

Signed values can be negative. This bridge reconciles the source to analytical net sales.

Definitions that matter

Net sales = gross positive sales − absolute negative-quantity credits. Gross AOV = gross sales ÷ distinct purchase invoices. Credit value ratio = credits ÷ gross sales.

Sales totals retain anonymous customers, postage and special codes. Merchandise charts use five-digit product codes with optional letter suffixes. Invalid dates, nonpositive prices, zero quantities and positive-quantity cancellation invoices are excluded.

Product costs, traffic and advertising spend are unavailable. This project does not estimate profit, conversion, ROAS or causal improvement.
